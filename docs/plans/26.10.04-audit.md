### Flake — root cause, from preserved daemon logs

Four runs of one unchanged binary: **2 pass, 2 fail**.

Failing run, daemon A log:

```
{"detail":"panel-b addr=127.0.0.1:41815 accept_only_ms=12000",
 "event":"peer_dial_deferred"}
{"detail":"ec6b41eb7d15 result=accept","event":"tls_verify_server"}
{"detail":"local-creator addr=inbound","event":"mesh_peer_connected"}
```

Daemon B log shows the same: TLS accepted, peer connected. **The mesh formed
successfully in the failing run.** The test still failed.

Cause: `bs-mesh-conn.h:824` `should_defer_outbound_for()` defers the outbound
dial for the larger-pubkey node and waits for an inbound, window
`kTieBreakAcceptWindowMs` = 12000 ms. The harness polls 300 times at 10 ms
(`tests/e2e/panel_session_acceptance.cpp:317`) and gives up at 3 s.

Product behaviour is correct. The test wait budget is below the product's own
connect window. The fix is the test.

### Corrections made during the audit

Three of my own intermediate conclusions were wrong and were corrected against
evidence rather than shipped:

1. Reported the three message types as declared-but-not-serialized. The
   serializers exist; the grep pattern was wrong.

   ephemeral ports and sets `listen_port` per daemon. Not the cause.
3. Reported the acceptance harness as failing. It passes roughly half the time.
   The real defect is a race, not a hard failure.
