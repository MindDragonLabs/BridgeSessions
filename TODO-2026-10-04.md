### B4. Fix the acceptance-harness flake

`tests/e2e/panel_session_acceptance.cpp` fails about half of all runs. Observed:
**2 pass, 2 fail** on one unchanged binary.

**Root cause — confirmed from preserved daemon logs, not inferred.** Both
daemons mesh successfully. The failure is a startup race in the product's
tie-break logic:

- `bs-mesh-conn.h:824` `should_defer_outbound_for()` — the node holding the
  **larger** pubkey logs `peer_dial_deferred accept_only_ms=12000` and waits for
  an inbound connection instead of dialling.
- The harness polls `SESSION_SCROLLBACK` 300 times at 10 ms = **3 seconds**
  (`tests/e2e/panel_session_acceptance.cpp:317`).

The test gives up at 3s while the product is still inside its own 12s accept
window. The product is correct; the test's wait budget is wrong.

**Do not** "fix" this by shortening `kTieBreakAcceptWindowMs`. That would weaken
real mesh behaviour to suit a test.

**Done means:** the wait budget exceeds the product connect window with margin,
10 consecutive runs pass, and the failure path still reports honestly instead of
hanging.

## Security items to close before any public write

### S1. Panel auth is new and unreviewed

`auth.py` and `invites.py` are new files, 14 dirty files, zero commits, never
audited. The plan already lists concrete concerns: cookies lack `Secure`; login
reads `Content-Length` with no size cap; password reset keeps the signing secret
and only clears the calling process's session map, so a CLI reset leaves
existing server sessions valid; invalid credential config silently makes
`login_enabled` false instead of failing closed; URL tokens persist in browser
history and logs; cookie sessions satisfy `require_token` including for invite
management.

**Done means:** each item above is fixed or explicitly declined with a reason in
this file. Not deferred silently.

### S2. Full mesh trust must not reach phones

A phone must never get mesh access merely to use chat, sessions, or files.

**Done means:** scoped per-device API credentials with enrolment, revocation,
expiry, and audit events are written down and enforced.

### S3. Privacy scrub must be re-verified at the integrated tree

Scrub is committed and clean in its own lane. It has **not** been re-run against
the integrated result.

**Done means:** `scripts/prepublish-scan.sh` clean on the integrated tree, with
its output saved.
